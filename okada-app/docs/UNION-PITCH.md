## OKADA BOSS
### Your members' papers, their daily money, and your dues — on one phone.

---

**Chairman, the law has changed.**

Under the Road Traffic Regulations, 2026 (L.I. 2519), your members now need a commercial
rider's licence, commercial registration and the yellow plate, roadworthiness, insurance, and
membership of a licensed organisation — yours. There is a transition window, and it is
closing. Riders who miss it face fines or worse.

Right now, most of your members do not know what they need, what it costs, or where to go.
And you have no list of who has sorted what.

---

## What we are offering the union — free

**1. A real member register.**
Every member on one list. Who has their licence. Whose insurance expires next month. Whose
roadworthiness is overdue. You open your phone and you know — instead of finding out when a
member is stopped at a barrier.

**2. Dues collected by MoMo, straight to the union's account.**
No cash. No collector walking the station. No argument about who paid and who did not. The
member pays from his phone, he gets a receipt, you see the total. Arrears are a list, not a
memory.

**3. A money book for every member.**
Fares in. Fuel, repairs, the owner's drop, dues — out. What is actually left at the end of the
day. Most of your members do not know this number. When they see it, they manage it. A member
who knows his numbers is a member who pays his dues.

**4. An emergency button.**
Crash or theft: one tap sends his location and bike details to his family and to you.

**It works without network and without data.** It is built for a cheap phone.

## What we do not ask

- **No money from the union. Not now, not at the pilot.**
- No exclusivity. Your members can leave whenever they want and take their records with them.
- No control of your account. Dues settle to the union's own MoMo number, not ours.

---

## How we make money later — so you know now

We will never charge a rider to record his own work. When the service is working and the union
is collecting through it, we take **a small percentage of dues collected** — one or two pesewas
in the cedi — and we earn commission when a member chooses insurance or a bike loan through
us. He chooses. Never automatic, never deducted without him agreeing.

**We want to tell you this at the start rather than have you find it out later.**

---

## Why this matters more than an app

A member on work-and-pay is riding another man's bike and will ride it for years. When he
records his takings every day, after six months he has something he has never had: **proof of
income**. That proof is what a lender needs to put the bike in his own name.

That is the real reason to do this. The dues collection is just what makes it pay for itself.
